1 Welcome to Albania Sky

Albania Sky is an online flight booking portal where you can search live flights, reserve seats, pay securely by card, and manage every booking in one place.

The portal is designed for two kinds of users:

Travelers

Individuals booking flights for themselves, family, or friends. You get instant booking references, secure card payment, and downloadable travel documents.

Travel Agents

Travel agencies booking on behalf of clients. Agents get special pricing/discounts, credit-based confirmation, and a transactions view to track price changes.

What you can doSearch flights · book with full passenger details and extras · pay in full or leave a balance · pay later by card · download tickets & vouchers · track payments and outstanding balances.

2 Getting Started & Language

Opening the portal

Open your web browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge, or Firefox) and go to:

albaniasky.com

The portal works on computers, tablets, and phones. No installation is required.

Choosing your language

Albania Sky is available in Albanian (SQ) and English (EN). Use the language switch in the top navigation bar to change it at any time. Your choice is remembered the next time you visit.

TipThe language toggle is in the top-right of every page. On a phone, open the menu (☰) to find it.

3 Creating an Account

You can browse and search flights without an account, but you need one to complete a booking and to access My Bookings.

  1. Click Register in the top navigation bar.
  2. Choose your account type at the top of the form: Traveler (individual) or Travel Agent.
  3. Fill in your details (see the table below).
  4. Click the register button. You can now log in and start booking.

4 Logging In

  1. Click Login in the top navigation bar.
  2. Enter your email and password. Use the eye icon to reveal the password if needed.
  3. Click Login. You are returned to the page you were on before.

When you are logged in, the navigation bar shows your name, a Agent badge (for agents), the My Bookings link, and a Logout button.

5 Searching for Flights

The search form on the home page is the starting point for every booking.

  1. From / To: Start typing a city or airport and pick it from the suggestions. The destination list updates to show only places Albania Sky flies to from your chosen origin.
  2. Date: Open the calendar and choose your travel date. Where available, the calendar shows the lowest price for each day so you can spot cheaper dates.
  3. Passengers: Set how many travelers are flying:
    • Adult — age 12 and over (at least 1 required)
    • Child — ages 2 to 11
    • Infant — under 2 years
  4. Click Search to see matching flights.
ShortcutThe home page also shows popular destinations with sample prices. Clicking one fills the search form for you and jumps straight to results.

6 Browsing Search Results

Each result shows the airline, flight number, departure and arrival times, the route, the date, and the price per adult.

  • Card or list view: Use the toggle at the top to switch between a visual card layout and a compact list.
  • Adjust your search: A compact search bar stays at the top so you can change cities, date, or passengers without going back.
  • Availability: A flight marked Sold Out has no seats left and cannot be selected.
  • Agent pricing: If you are logged in as an agent, your discounted price is shown next to the standard price.

When you find the right flight, click Select to continue to the booking form.

7 Booking a Flight

On the booking page you enter passenger details, choose any extras, and confirm.

Step 1 — Passenger details

Complete a section for each traveler. Fields include:

  • First name and Last name — exactly as in the passport
  • Passport number
  • Nationality
  • Date of birth
ImportantNames must match the travel document exactly. Airlines may refuse boarding if the name on the ticket does not match the passport.

Step 2 — Extras (optional)

For each passenger you can add: checked baggage, seat selection, priority boarding, and travel insurance. Extras are added to the total automatically.

Step 3 — Contact information

Provide a contact name, email, and phone so we can reach you about the booking and send confirmations.

Step 4 — Confirm

You have two ways to finish, depending on whether you want to pay now:

ButtonWhat it does
Confirm BookingCreates the booking and holds it. The status becomes Pending (or Confirmed for agents booking on credit). You can pay later from the booking details.
Pay and BookCreates the booking and immediately opens secure card payment (POK) to pay in full now.
Not logged in?If you start a booking without being logged in, your details are saved and you are taken to the login page. After signing in, your booking continues right where you left off.

After a successful booking you receive a booking reference and can download your ticket immediately or go to My Bookings.

8 Paying for Your Booking

Albania Sky accepts secure online card payments through the POK payment gateway. Your card details are entered on POK's secure page — Albania Sky never stores your card number.

Where you can pay by card

PlaceWhen the card option appears
During booking — Pay and BookRight after you create a new booking, to pay in full immediately.
Booking details — Pay by CardOn an existing booking whenever there is an outstanding balance to pay.

How card payment works

  1. Click Pay and Book or Pay by Card.
  2. You are redirected to the secure POK payment page.
  3. Enter your card details and confirm the payment.
  4. You are returned to Albania Sky, where the payment is verified automatically.
  5. On success you see a confirmation with the amount paid and the updated balance. If a payment fails, you can simply try again.
Secured by POK Pay · SSLAll card payments are processed over an encrypted connection by the POK payment gateway.

Paying part now, part later

You don't always have to pay the full amount at once. A booking can have a remaining balance, which you can settle later by card from the booking details page (see Section 10).

9 My Bookings

Open My Bookings from the navigation bar to see and manage everything you have booked.

Summary at a glance

The top of the page summarizes your activity: total number of bookings, total amount, amount already paid, and the outstanding balance still due.

The bookings list

Each row shows:

  • Reference — the unique booking code
  • Customer and route
  • Travel date
  • Status — Draft, Pending, Confirmed, Ticketed, or Cancelled
  • Amount and any balance due

Click any booking to expand a quick financial summary (with a paid/remaining progress bar) and its payment history. To open the full page, go into the booking details.

10 Booking Details & Paying a Balance

Click a booking to open its full details page. This is where you review everything and pay any remaining balance.

What the details page shows

  • Header — booking reference, PNR (airline reference, when available), and the current status.
  • Flight information — route, airline, times, and airport codes.
  • Passengers — each traveler with their baggage, seat, and other extras.
  • Financial summary — total amount, amount paid, and balance due.
  • Payment history — every payment recorded against the booking.

Paying the remaining balance

When a booking still has money owing, a purple Pay by Card button appears in the financial summary.

  1. Open the booking from My Bookings.
  2. Check the Remaining amount in the financial summary.
  3. Click Pay by Card to pay the balance securely via POK (see Section 8).
  4. After payment, the balance updates and the booking shows as settled when fully paid.
Good to knowThe Pay by Card button only appears when there is a balance owing and the booking is not cancelled. A fully paid booking shows as Settled.

11 Tickets & Vouchers

Albania Sky gives you two printable documents. Both can be saved as PDF or printed from your browser.

Ticket

A detailed document with the flight breakdown and price totals (ticket fare, taxes, extras, and any agent discount). Use this as your booking record.

Voucher (Booking Confirmation)

A clean, branded confirmation showing the PNR and who is traveling. It clearly states that it is not a boarding pass.

How to download or print

  1. Open the booking (after booking, or from My Bookings).
  2. Click the Download / Print option for the ticket or voucher.
  3. In the print window, choose your printer — or select Save as PDF to keep a digital copy.
RememberThe voucher is a confirmation, not a boarding pass. You still check in with the airline and obtain a boarding pass before your flight.

12 For Travel Agents

Agent accounts unlock features tailored to agencies:

  • Special pricing — discounted fares (fixed amount or percentage) are shown alongside standard prices in search results.
  • Credit booking — eligible agents can confirm bookings on credit rather than paying immediately.
  • Price-change tracking — the Price Changes tab in My Bookings lists increases and decreases applied to your bookings.
  • Agency details — your agency name and NIPT are kept on your profile for invoicing.
ActivationIf agent pricing or credit options are missing, your account may still be pending activation by an administrator. Contact support to confirm.

13 Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an account to search flights?

No. You can search and browse freely. An account is needed only to complete a booking and to use My Bookings.

My card payment failed — what now?

Nothing is lost. Return to the booking and click Pay by Card again. If it keeps failing, check your card details and available funds, or contact support.

Can I pay later instead of all at once?

Yes. Use Confirm Booking to reserve, then pay the balance by card later from the booking details page.

Is my card information safe?

Yes. Card details are entered on the secure POK payment page over an encrypted (SSL) connection. Albania Sky does not store your card number.

I can't see my booking.

Make sure you are logged in with the same email used to make the booking, then open My Bookings.

The name on a passenger is wrong.

Contact support as soon as possible. Name corrections may be subject to airline rules and fees.

Why is a flight marked "Sold Out"?

All seats for that flight are taken. Try a different date or a nearby route.
